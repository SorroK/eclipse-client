Eclipse HUD's motion blur implementation is a port of the velocity
reprojection technique from Smooth Motion Blur by Jahleel:

  https://github.com/realjahleel/smooth-motion-blur

which in turn adapts Natural Motion Blur by ItsPasi:

  https://github.com/ItsPasi/natural-motionblur-fabric
